You own a farm in a fantasy realm
You farm magic plants and animals and sell these goods for gold

# Producers
Farms of animals and plants
These can be sold directly or used to produce more expensive resources

### Lumber Yard
Creates (6) Wood per tick
Can not be sold
Used to build and upgrade every other building in the game
Upgraded by all the other resources in the game
Costs 100 Credits to make

### Slime Ranch
Generates (5) gel per tick
Sells for (1) gold
Costs 50 Credits and 10 Wood to make

### Goblin Hut
Generates (4) Goblin Ears per tick
Costs 100 Credits and 20 Wood to make

### Ork Grotto
Generates (3) Ork Hide per tick
Costs 150 Credits and 30 Wood to make

### Imp Pit
Generates (2) Imp Horns per tick
Costs 200 Credits and 40 Wood to make

### Dragon Lair
Generates (1) Dragon Scale per tick
Costs 300 Credits and 60 Wood to make

# Converters
Takes in materials to create a new product

### Kitchen
Takes in Gel (8) and Goblin Ears (6) to cook
Outputs Goblin Skewers
Costs 150 Credits and 30 Wood to make

### Tailor
Takes in Gel (15) and Orc Hide (7)
Outputs Orken Jacket
Costs 200 Credits and 40 Wood to make

### Weapon Forge
Takes in Gel (20) and Imp Horns (5)
Outputs  Hellfire Daggers
Costs 250 Credits and 50 Wood to make

### Armour Forge
Takes in Gel (20) or Dragon Scales (4)
Outputs  Dragon Scale Armour
Costs 300 Credits and 60 Wood to make

# Consumers
Can be used to sell goods
Themed shops based on the different resources types

### Gel Shop
Sells (2) gel per tick 
Gives (1) gold per gel
Costs 50 Credits and 15 Wood to make

### Restaurant
Sells (1) Goblin Skewers
Can be sold for (18) gold
Costs 150 Credits and 25 Wood to make

### Clothing Store
Sells (1) Orken Jackets
Can be sold for (33) gold
Costs 200 Credits and 35 Wood to make

### Weapon Shop
Sells (1) Hellfire Daggers
Can be sold for (40) gold
Costs 250 Credits and 50 Wood to make

### Armoury
Sells (1) Dragon Scale Armour
Can be sold for (50) gold
Costs 300 Credits and 60 Wood to make
